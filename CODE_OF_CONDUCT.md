# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and patient. Discuss the work rather than the person.
Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate exposure of private
data, and sustained disruption are not accepted.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation to keep
the project safe and constructive. Report conduct concerns through the same
private channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), without including
unrelated personal information.
